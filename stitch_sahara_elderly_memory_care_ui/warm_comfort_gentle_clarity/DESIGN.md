## Brand & Style

The design style marries soft organic tactile elements with an accessible, high-contrast visual architecture. Designed specifically for elderly individuals experiencing mild-to-moderate dementia, their family members, and caregivers across North East India, the visual presence is dignified, deeply calming, and distinctly non-clinical.

### Personality and Emotional Pillars
- **Dignity & Warmth (`Aadarna`):** The tone treats the elder as a cherished matriarch or patriarch (e.g., "Asha ji", "Bhaben da"), rejecting clinical sterility in favor of familial warmth.
- **Cognitive Ease & Serenity:** Visual noise, dense information hierarchies, and jarring transitions are eliminated. Gentle forest and tea-garden greens invoke the natural landscapes of Assam, Meghalaya, and the surrounding hill states.
- **Physical Reassurance:** Every interaction behaves like a solid, physical button or card—invoking tangible touch surfaces rather than flat, abstract digital screens.

## Colors

The color system prioritizes clear visual thresholds, warm herbal cues, and strict WCAG AAA contrast compliance for aging retinas and low-contrast sensitivity.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Deep Green (`#2E7D32`):** Grounded botanical green for primary actions, top headers, strong landmarks, and major navigation anchors.
- **Bright Action Green (`#43A047`):** High-energy conversational affirmative cues, call-to-action cards, audio recording triggers, and interactive states.
- **Soft Mint Background Canvas (`#EAF7EC`):** A restful, glare-free overall screen backdrop that significantly reduces ocular fatigue compared to harsh digital white.
- **Light Green Cards (`#F5FBF6`):** Secondary container surface providing subtle grouping without visual clutter.
- **White Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority focus areas, active game tiles, and memory card content.
- **Dark Readable Text (`#17351B`):** Deep organic pine green-black yielding extreme legibility across all mint and white surfaces (>10:1 contrast ratio).
- **Secondary Muted Text (`#5B6B5D`):** Mid-tone olive-slate for auxiliary labels, timestamps, and caregiver metadata.
- **Soft Warning Amber (`#F6B73C`):** Gentle reminder state for medication schedules and pending caregiver alerts.
- **Gentle Alert Red (`#D9534F`):** Non-punitive, warm coral-crimson for misses, emergencies, or uncompleted daily tasks.

## Typography

The design system exclusively adopts **Plus Jakarta Sans** for its combination of rounded, friendly terminals, generous aperture openings, and high x-height. This ensures superior letter differentiation (e.g., distinguishing 'c', 'e', and 'o') critical for users with cataracts, macular degeneration, or cognitive processing slowdowns.

### Typographic Principles
- **Floor Limit:** No functional text anywhere drops below `14px`, and no interactive or instructional text drops below `16px`.
- **Generous Leading:** Paragraphs and multi-line task prompts maintain a minimum 1.5x to 1.6x line-height to prevent track-skipping during reading.
- **Conversational Salience:** Headings incorporate generous weights (`700` and `800`) with warm, informal phrasing, providing clear conversational signposts across the screen.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first, single-column or gentle 2-column layout model is enforced. Content never requires lateral scanning or complex eye tracking.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile Handheld (320px - 600px):** Single column with `1.25rem` (20px) outer edge margins. Cards stretch edge-to-edge within safe margins.
- **Tablet & Caregiver Surface (601px - 1024px):** Max container width capped at `48rem` (768px), centered on screen with `2rem` (32px) margins to keep sightlines tight and avoid physical reach fatigue.
- **Desktop Dashboard (>1024px):** Caregiver companion mode splits into a primary activity column and an auxiliary support sidebar; patient mode remains centered in a distraction-free tablet format.

### Spacing Rhythms & Touch Boundaries
- All interactive targets adhere to a strict minimum physical footprint of **48px x 48px**, with **56px to 72px** prioritized for game pieces, confirmation actions, and audio replay triggers.
- Spacing between consecutive interactive elements must be no less than `1rem` (16px) to eliminate accidental tremors and erroneous taps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile physical metaphors rather than abstract elevation. Elements feel like physical smooth wooden or cardstock blocks placed on a soft mint mat.

### Tonal Stratification
1. **Base Layer (`#EAF7EC`):** The foundational quiet canvas.
2. **Resting Container (`#F5FBF6`):** Secondary cards, day logs, and reminder groups sitting flush or with an ultra-subtle border stroke (`1px solid #D7EAD9`).
3. **Tactile Interactive Cards (`#FFFFFF`):** Primary memory games, photo cards, and daily questions sit raised with an ambient drop: `0px 4px 12px rgba(23, 53, 27, 0.07)`.
4. **Action Buttons & Active Targets:** Raised, pill-shaped or rounded blocks utilizing a directional physical shadow: `0px 6px 0px #1E5622` (primary) or `0px 6px 0px #2F7232` (bright action). On touch/press, the element translates downward by `3px` with a diminished shadow (`0px 3px 0px`), delivering immediate mechanical clarity.

## Shapes

Shapes feature organic, broad curves that convey safety, soft contact, and approachability. 

### Geometric Rules
- **Memory & Content Cards:** Constructed with broad `20px` to `24px` radii (`rounded-xl`), removing harsh corners and guiding the eye gently into the interior photo or text.
- **Buttons:** Large buttons feature full pill curvature (`9999px`) or `16px` to `20px` rounded rectangles, accommodating comfortable finger placement.
- **Status Indicators & Badges:** Rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) housing generous text padding and supportive iconography.

## Components

### Buttons & Call-to-Actions
- **Primary Hero Button:** Minimum height `56px`, pill-shaped (`9999px`), background `#2E7D32`, text `#FFFFFF` in `action-lg` (`22px` bold). Accompanied by prominent left-aligned tactile iconography (e.g., green speaker icon for voice prompts).
- **Secondary Action Button:** Minimum height `56px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `2px solid #2E7D32`, text `#2E7D32`.
- **Audio Guidance Trigger:** Floating or inline circular audio prompt button (`56px x 56px`), `#43A047` fill, white speaker symbol, labeled clearly with "Suniyé" / "Listen".

### Cards & Memory Tiles
- **Memory Deck Tile:** `#FFFFFF` background, `24px` rounded corners, padding `1.5rem`. Framed by a gentle `1.5px` border in `#D7EAD9`. Holds large family photographs with rounded `16px` inner frames, accompanied by `headline-sm` titles and audio playback controls.
- **Daily Rhythm Card:** `#F5FBF6` surface with a vertical accent bar on the left edge (`6px` width, color-coded by time of day: morning sun `#F6B73C`, midday `#43A047`, evening `#2E7D32`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Height `36px` to `40px`, padding `0.5rem 1.25rem`. Background `#EAF7EC` with `#2E7D32` text for routine updates, `#FEF6E7` with `#9A6700` text for gentle reminders, and `#FBEAEA` with `#992622` text for urgent alerts.

### Interactive Choice Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Large Selection Radio:** Replaced with full selectable tactile tiles. Minimum height `64px`, border `2px solid #D7EAD9`, padding `1rem 1.25rem`. When selected, the border turns to `3px solid #2E7D32` with a `#F5FBF6` fill and a large checkmark icon (`28px`).

### Input Fields
- Form interactions are kept minimal. When required, inputs possess a minimum height of `56px`, bold `18px` text, a `2px solid #5B6B5D` resting border, and a prominent green halo focus ring (`3px solid #43A047`). Placeholders display high contrast rather than faint grey.

### Audio & Caregiver Floating Anchors
- **Caregiver SOS & Direct Voice Link:** Persistently visible, non-obtrusive bar docked at the base of the screen with a `56px` quick-tap direct connection to a primary family caregiver.
